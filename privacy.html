Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

This document describes what CVMatch actually collects and does with it, based on the real system — not a generic template. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It's written with South Africa's POPIA in mind, but you should have it properly reviewed before relying on it for a public launch with real user data.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, name, password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text)Account creation and login
Your uploaded CV, pasted job descriptions, and the tailored CVs / cover letters / other documents CVMatch generates for youThe actual service — analysis, tailoring, generation
Application tracker entries you create (company, role, status, dates, notes)The job application tracker feature
Subscription status and a PayFast-issued reference tokenKnowing which plan you're on. We never see or store your card number — PayFast handles that directly.

We don't collect data we don't need. There's no advertising tracker, no behavioral analytics pixel, and no sale of your data to third parties, anywhere in CVMatch.

2. Third parties that process your data

Processing your data through CVMatch necessarily involves a small number of specialist providers:

ProviderWhat they seeWhy
Groq (AI inference)Your CV content and job description text, when you request an analysis or generationThey run the AI model that powers CVMatch's core features
PayFast (payments)Your name, email, and payment detailsProcessing subscription payments. We never receive your card number.
Resend (email delivery)Your email address, for transactional emails only (verification, account notices)Sending you account-related email

We don't share your CV content, job search activity, or personal details with any other third party, including other users, recruiters, or advertisers.

3. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your uploaded files, generated documents, and tracker entries are removed. Billing records are kept for 5 years after account closure, in line with South African tax record-keeping requirements.

4. Your rights

Under POPIA (and similar frameworks if you're outside South Africa), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information (most of this you can edit directly in your account)
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Object to processing, or ask us what legal basis we're relying on for a specific use of your data

To exercise any of these, contact us at kmajeza619@gmail.com.

5. Security

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Connections to CVMatch are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your account data is restricted to what's needed to operate the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we'll notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

6. Children

CVMatch isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CVMatch changes. Material changes will be posted here with an updated date.
