## YOUR WORKFLOW (step-by-step using the app)

Follow this every time you receive a Fiverr order:

**Step 1 — Receive the order**
- Client sends their CV + job posting
- Note their name and preferred cover letter tone

**Step 2 — Set up the app**
- Open the Job Application Assistant (double-click desktop icon)
- Enter your Groq API key if not already saved
- Go to "New Application"

**Step 3 — Upload the CV**
- Upload the client's CV (PDF or DOCX) using "Upload New CV"
- Select it from the list

**Step 4 — Add the job description**
- Paste the job description text OR enter the URL to fetch it automatically

**Step 5 — Analyze**
- Click "Analyze Job" and wait (~15 seconds)
- Review the extracted skills, keywords, company name, and job title
- For Standard/Premium: also click "ATS Score" and "Match Score" — save those results to share with client

**Step 6 — Generate documents**
- Enter the job title and company name (confirm from analysis)
- Enter the client's full name
- Select the cover letter tone (ask client, or default to "Formal")
- Click "Generate CV + Cover Letter"

**Step 7 — Review & adjust**
- Click "Preview CV" and "Preview Cover Letter" to read through
- Download both documents
- Make any manual tweaks in Word if needed (names, dates, etc.)

**Step 8 — Deliver on Fiverr**
- Upload the Word and PDF versions
- Write a short delivery message (template below)

**Step 9 — For Premium orders also:**
- Click "Interview Prep" for questions → copy and paste into a Word doc
- Use the Company Research button → copy into a doc
- Use Follow-up Email button → deliver as a template
- Use Salary Negotiation button → deliver as a PDF/Word doc
